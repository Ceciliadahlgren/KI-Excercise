Teacher's notes (click to expand)

Group sizes and risk: A: 10 people, $100 expected annual cost, max willingness to pay $250. B: 5 people, $400 expected cost, max $550. C: 5 people, $1,000 expected cost, max $1,400.

Typical spiral: $400 with A+B+C → A leaves → $700 with B+C → B leaves → $1,000 with C only.

Rules: Groups make one collective decision. Once a group leaves, it cannot return in this run. The insurer breaks even on expected medical costs, with no administrative expenses, profit or subsidies. A full collapse also occurs if all groups leave.

Facilitation: Do not tell students which choice to make. Ask them to explain their reasoning. After the demonstration, discuss adverse selection, community rating, mandates, subsidies and risk pooling. This model is intentionally simplified, not a prediction of actual insurance markets.
